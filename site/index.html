Talk to your own OpenClaw assistant from your iPhone. Speak naturally, hear replies, or type when you prefer.

Voxkin is currently in beta. A compatible, reachable OpenClaw gateway is required. There is no vendor-operated Voxkin conversation backend.

iPhone client. Flexible gateway hosting.

Tested beta gateway configurations include macOS on Apple Silicon, Ubuntu 24.04 x86_64 containers, and native Windows 11 x64—without patching OpenClaw. Debian voice-runtime tests also passed. See platform requirements and the testing matrix for exact coverage and limitations.

Download the optional gateway plugin for local voices and restricted guest phones.

Built for your setup

  • Connect directly to your gateway, which chooses the agent and speech providers.
  • Use on-device dictation when supported by your iPhone and selected language.
  • Optional gateway plugin support for English and Spanish voices and separately enrolled phones.

Restricted-phone access is experimental and conversation-only in this beta.

Voice conversations with CarPlay

Talk to your agent through CarPlay on iOS 26.4 or later. Start conversations explicitly, hear spoken replies, and stop or cancel from the CarPlay interface. Complete gateway setup on your iPhone before driving.
