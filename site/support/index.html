Support

Get connected.

What you need

Voxkin requires an OpenClaw gateway you own or have permission to use. Installing the app alone does not create an assistant or grant gateway access.

Owner connection

Use your gateway’s HTTPS/WSS address and the appropriate owner credentials. Approve device pairing through your gateway’s administration workflow. Never share an administrator token with another phone user.

Invited phones

Your gateway owner sends a short-lived Voxkin invitation. Open or paste it in the app, confirm the destination, enroll, and wait for the owner to approve your device. An expired invitation must be replaced. TestFlight installation and gateway enrollment are separate steps.

Connection or speech problems

  • Confirm the gateway is running and reachable from your current network. If it uses a private network, join that network first.
  • Check microphone and speech-recognition permissions in iOS Settings.
  • For Spanish, select the appropriate language and an available compatible voice. On-device recognition must be available.
  • If access was revoked, contact the gateway owner; the app cannot override that decision.

Report a problem

For app support, email jason@builderofthings.studio. During TestFlight testing, you can also use TestFlight’s Send Beta Feedback feature. Include the app version, iOS version, expected behavior and steps to reproduce. Do not include gateway tokens, invitations, private conversations or other credentials.

Contact jason@builderofthings.studio for Voxkin support. For server access or conversation deletion, contact your gateway administrator.
