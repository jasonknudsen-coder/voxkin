Privacy

Your data’s route, explained.

Updated October 15, 2026. This page describes the Voxkin iPhone app and this website, not the independent policies of your gateway operator or its service providers.

Speech and conversations

Voxkin uses Apple on-device speech recognition where available and does not enable cloud recognition fallback. Recognized text is sent to the gateway you configure. That gateway may send text to model providers, speech providers, or other services according to its configuration.

Replies are spoken through the gateway’s configured speech service or an optional local voice plugin. A direct gateway connection does not mean every downstream service runs locally.

Storage and credentials

The app does not save transcript or raw audio files by default. Gateway conversation history and logs follow the gateway operator’s policies. Clearing the phone’s view does not delete gateway records.

Connection credentials are stored in device-only iOS Keychain storage. Preferences may be saved separately. Forgetting a connection removes local credentials; ask the gateway administrator to revoke access or delete server-side data.

Your gateway and our review environment

Normal use requires a gateway you own or have permission to use; the app does not include a hosted AI service. The app includes no advertising SDK or app analytics SDK.

If we separately give you access to our isolated review or test gateway, we operate that environment. It may retain submitted text, replies, device/access identifiers and technical logs, and sends text to its configured model provider to answer requests. Do not submit personal or sensitive information to that environment. Access expiration does not itself delete stored records. Contact us to request deletion of data held in that environment; downstream providers apply their own retention policies.

Apple may independently process diagnostic or TestFlight feedback data under its own settings and policies. If you voluntarily send support email or beta feedback, we use the information you provide to investigate and respond. Do not include credentials or private conversations.

This website

This static site uses no analytics scripts, advertising, or application cookies. Hosting and network infrastructure may process IP addresses, requested URLs and technical request information to deliver and protect the site.

Questions and requests

For conversation deletion or gateway privacy questions, contact your gateway administrator. See support for troubleshooting. For publisher privacy inquiries, email jason@builderofthings.studio.
