Privacy

Your data’s route, explained.

Updated October 20, 2026. This page describes the Voxkin iPhone and Android apps and this website, not the independent policies of your gateway operator or its service providers.

Speech and conversations

Voxkin uses platform on-device speech recognition: Apple recognition on iPhone and Android on-device recognition on supported Android devices with the selected language model installed. The app does not enable cloud recognition fallback. Recognized text is sent to the gateway you configure. That gateway may send text to model providers, speech providers, or other services according to its configuration.

Replies are spoken through the gateway’s configured speech service or an optional local voice plugin. A direct gateway connection does not mean every downstream service runs locally.

Storage and credentials

The app does not save raw microphone recordings or transcript files by default. Android temporarily caches generated reply audio for playback; files are removed when playback completes or stops normally. If the app is interrupted before cleanup, temporary files may remain in its private cache until removed by the operating system or app-data removal. Gateway conversation history and logs follow the gateway operator’s policies. Clearing the phone’s view does not delete gateway records.

On iPhone, connection credentials are stored in device-only iOS Keychain storage. On Android, credentials are encrypted with AES-GCM using keys protected by Android Keystore, and app backup is disabled. Preferences may be saved separately. Forgetting a connection removes local credentials; ask the gateway administrator to revoke access or delete server-side data.

Optional Android response reports

If you choose Report response and then Submit report, the previewed response text, selected reason, optional note and a random report reference are sent to Builder of Things Studio. You can edit or remove the response text before submitting. No other messages, microphone audio, gateway address or credentials are automatically attached. Normal conversations still go directly to your gateway.

We use reports to investigate unsafe or offensive AI output, not for advertising. Reports are retained for up to 30 days. Our receiving host handles your network address for delivery and temporary in-memory rate limiting, but does not attach it to report records. Reporting and retries are manual. Contact support with your report reference to request earlier deletion.

Your gateway and our review environment

Normal use requires a gateway you own or have permission to use; the app does not include a hosted AI service. The app includes no advertising SDK or app analytics SDK.

If we separately give you access to our isolated review or test gateway, we operate that environment. It may retain submitted text, replies, device/access identifiers and technical logs, and sends text to its configured model provider to answer requests. Do not submit personal or sensitive information to that environment. Access expiration does not itself delete stored records. Contact us to request deletion of data held in that environment; downstream providers apply their own retention policies.

Apple and Google may independently process store, diagnostic or testing-feedback data under their own settings and policies. If you voluntarily send support email or beta feedback, we use the information you provide to investigate and respond. Do not include credentials or private conversations.

This website

This static site uses no analytics scripts, advertising, or application cookies. Hosting and network infrastructure may process IP addresses, requested URLs and technical request information to deliver and protect the site.

Questions and requests

For conversation deletion or gateway privacy questions, contact your gateway administrator. See support for troubleshooting. For publisher privacy inquiries, email jason@builderofthings.studio.
